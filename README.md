# RePlay India

## The six screens

1. **Prologue** introduces the idea and visual identity.
2. **The Journey** shows the complete Forgotten, Found, and Forward path.
3. **Forgotten** contains four small simulations based on traditional games.
4. **Found** contains modern field-game challenges.
5. **Forward** presents the games inside a futuristic console interface.
6. **Legacy** shows points, badges, progress, and the RePlay pledge.

Every screen fits inside one device viewport. The app does not use a long vertical page.

## Playable
Choose cricket, football, or hockey. Move the aim slider toward the glowing target, then take the shot.


## Controls

- Tap the bottom arrows to change screens.
- Tap any bottom dot to jump to a screen.
- Swipe left or right on mobile.
- Use the left and right arrow keys on a computer.
- Press Escape to close the future game overlay.
