# RePlay India


The complete story follows:

**Forgotten → Found → Forward**



## How to run it

1. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` in the same folder.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## The six screens

1. **Prologue** introduces the idea and visual identity.
2. **The Journey** shows the complete Forgotten, Found, and Forward path.
3. **Forgotten** contains four small simulations based on traditional games.
4. **Found** contains modern field-game challenges.
5. **Forward** presents the games inside a futuristic console interface.
6. **Legacy** shows points, badges, progress, and the RePlay pledge.

Every screen fits inside one device viewport. The app does not use a long vertical page.

## Playable experiences

### Forgotten

- **Gilli-Danda:** stop the moving marker near the middle for a strong strike.
- **Lattoo:** launch a spinning wooden top.
- **Stapoo:** tap the glowing squares from one to seven.
- **Lagori:** throw a ball at the stack of seven stones.

### Found

Choose cricket, football, or hockey. Move the aim slider toward the glowing target, then take the shot.

### Forward

Choose one of four futuristic game concepts and launch it. Tap when the moving light reaches the top of the portal.

## Points and progress

Playing challenges adds heritage points. The app saves progress in the browser with `localStorage`.

The three badges are:

- **Memory Keeper:** play two forgotten games
- **Field Hero:** complete a present-day field challenge
- **Future Player:** launch a futuristic arcade remix

The final pledge completes the full journey.

## Files

- `index.html` contains the six screens and their content.
- `style.css` contains the responsive layout, artwork, console design, and animations.
- `script.js` controls navigation, mini-games, scoring, badges, and saved progress.
- `README.md` explains the project in simple language.

## Changing the project


The forgotten-game text is inside `oldGameData` in `script.js`. The future arcade games are inside `futureGames` in the same file



## Controls

- Tap the bottom arrows to change screens.
- Tap any bottom dot to jump to a screen.
- Swipe left or right on mobile.
- Use the left and right arrow keys on a computer.
- Press Escape to close the future game overlay.
